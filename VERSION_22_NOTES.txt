SKY'S BRIDGE VERSION 22

- Adds optional date of birth and date of passing to memorial submissions.
- Birthday: the child's star shines brighter and sparkles each year.
- Date of passing: a quiet animated candle appears beside the star each year.

- Memorial pop-up displays the entered dates.
- Includes an accessibility reduced-motion fallback.
