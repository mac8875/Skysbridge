Community Guidelines

Together, we create a safe and compassionate space for everyone.

♡

Be kind and respectful

A safe space

No bullying, harassment or hate speech will be tolerated.

▣

Protect privacy

Respect the privacy of others. Do not share personal information or private stories without permission.

◯

Share mindfully

Share your story only if and when you feel ready. Never pressure another person to share.

✦

Honour every memory

All memorials and stories are valued. We honour every child and every journey.

⚑

Report concerns

If you see harmful, unsafe or inappropriate behaviour, please let us know.

Thank you for helping us build a community rooted in love, understanding and hope.
