Community Guidelines

This space is carried by care: for every child, every story and every person who enters it.

Be kind and respectful

Meet every person and every story with kindness, patience and respect.

Protect privacy

Never share another person's identity, story or content without permission.

Share mindfully

Share only when ready and never pressure another person to speak.

Honour every memory

Every child matters. Every family grieves differently. Every memory deserves dignity.

No harassment or hate

Bullying, discrimination, threats and exploitation are not tolerated.

Report concerns

Contact together@skysbridge.org when something feels unsafe.
