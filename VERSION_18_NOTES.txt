Sky's Bridge Version 18

- Optional email copies for messages posted in selected protected rooms.
- Secure Netlify server function; no mail credentials are exposed in the browser.
- Destination is configured with ROOM_MESSAGE_FORWARD_TO in Netlify.
- Clear in-room disclosure when forwarding is enabled.
- Duplicate-email protection through email_forwarded_at.

Required:
1. Run V18_RUN_THIS_IN_SUPABASE.sql once.
2. In Netlify environment variables set ROOM_MESSAGE_FORWARD_TO to your Microsoft 365 mailbox.
3. Ensure RESEND_API_KEY and MAIL_FROM are configured and the sending domain is verified.
