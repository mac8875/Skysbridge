Established support outside Skysbridge

Professional help should come from qualified, established services.

Skysbridge is a remembrance platform. It does not provide therapy, diagnosis, clinical advice or therapist matching. The organisations below offer bereavement information, peer support or routes toward appropriate local help.

Local support

United Arab Emirates

Bereavement support, pregnancy and infant-loss resources, and clinically guided grief services available in or from the UAE.

Grief consultations and groups · UAE

Raymee Grief Services

Free one-to-one grief consultations and clinician-led support groups for individuals and families, provided through The LightHouse Arabia.

Pregnancy, neonatal and infant loss · UAE

A UAE-founded, not-for-profit community supporting families through high-risk pregnancy, premature birth, neonatal journeys and loss, including its Love Through Loss resources.

National and regional support

Germany

Self-help networks, bereavement services and specialist organisations for parents, siblings, children and families after the death of a child.

Bereaved parents and siblings · Germany

VEID e.V.

A national association connecting bereaved parents and siblings with regional groups, information, peer support and compassionate guidance.

Visit the official website →

Pregnancy and baby loss · Germany

Initiative REGENBOGEN

A parent-led initiative for families who have lost a child before, during or shortly after birth, with information and contacts for support groups.

Visit the official website →

Child hospice and family support · Germany

Frag OSKAR

The support portal of the Bundesverband Kinderhospiz, offering telephone, online and information services for families affected by a child's life-limiting illness, dying or bereavement.

Visit the official website →

Bereaved children and young people · Germany

Trauerland

Support for bereaved children, teenagers, young adults and their families through groups, counselling, information and educational resources.

Visit the official website →

Sudden infant death · Germany

GEPS Deutschland e.V.

A parent self-help organisation supporting families affected by sudden infant death, including information, personal exchange and contact with other bereaved parents.

Visit the official website →

Early child loss · Hesse and Rhine-Main

Unsere Sternenkinder Hessen e.V.

Free, professionally supported groups, bereavement guidance and practical information for families affected by pregnancy loss, stillbirth or the death of a baby.

Visit the official website →
