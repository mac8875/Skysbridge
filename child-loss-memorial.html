Remembering a child

A child remains part of a family.

When a child dies, love does not end. Their name, their story and their place in the family remain. Skysbridge is a dignified digital memorial where parents can preserve what matters and, if they choose, give their child a visible place among the stars.

A place for names and stories

A memorial after child loss or baby loss

There is no single way to keep a child close. Some parents speak their child's name often. Others keep photographs, letters, small objects or private rituals. Some families want a lasting place where a child's name and story can remain visible. Skysbridge was created to respect these different ways of remembering.

Grief after the death of a child is personal

Child loss does not follow a fixed timetable. Some people want connection; others need quiet. Skysbridge does not tell families how to grieve. It offers a place for remembrance and an optional protected community.

Skysbridge is not a therapy provider and does not replace professional care. People looking for additional support can use our professional support resources.

The heart of Skysbridge

Create a memorial star for a child

Parents can create a memorial page for their child and decide what remains private. A family may also request a place in the public star-filled sky. Public memorials are personally reviewed before publication.

Ways to keep a child's memory present

There is no right or wrong form of remembrance. Families may choose to:

  • say and preserve their child's name,
  • collect memories, letters or stories,
  • mark birthdays and meaningful dates in their own way,
  • create a star or another lasting memorial place,
  • connect with other bereaved parents when that feels helpful.

On Skysbridge, a public memorial star can shine a little brighter on a child's birthday. On the anniversary of their death, a candle can appear as a quiet sign of remembrance.

For bereaved parents, without rankings or comparison

Grief is not a popularity contest. Skysbridge has no likes or popularity rankings around a child's memorial. The dignity of each child and the choices of each family come first.

Why Skysbridge began

Skysbridge began with my son Sky. The love for him became the beginning of a place where no child is reduced to a statistic, a date or a silence. A Sky Full of Stars preserves names and stories for the families who choose to make them visible.
