How Skysbridge works

A place for their name. A place for their story. A place to remember.

Skysbridge was created so that no child is forgotten. Creating a memorial takes only a few minutes, and you remain in control of whether it stays private or is considered for the public Wall of Stars.

Four simple steps

Remember in the way that feels right to you.

1

Tell us about your child

Share your child's name and a remembrance. Dates and country are optional. You decide how much you want to tell.

2

Choose whether their star is public

Your submission stays private unless you request a place on the public Wall of Stars. Public memorials are reviewed by a person before they appear.

3

Special days remain special

On your child's birthday, their star shines a little brighter. On the anniversary of their passing, a candle appears in remembrance.

4

Return whenever you want

Your child's place can be visited and shared with the people you choose. You may also ask us to correct or delete a memorial by contacting Skysbridge.

Privacy and dignity

Your story belongs to you.

Accounts and memorial submissions remain private unless publication is clearly requested. There are no likes, rankings or popularity around a child's memory, and Skysbridge does not place advertising around grief.
