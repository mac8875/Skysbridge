# Sky’s Bridge — Version 2.1

Adds Spanish alongside German and English across the seven public pages, sign-in, memorial submission, Sky story and memories, and member-area interface. Language switching retains the corresponding page and home section. Family-written stories and posts remain in their original language. Existing privacy controls, review requirements, star centring and remembrance-date rules are preserved.

The Version 2.0 source backups remain available in the existing backup branches.
