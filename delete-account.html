Request account deletion

To request deletion of your Skysbridge account and associated personal data, send us an email from the address registered to your account. We will verify the request and process the deletion. Your request can include your account, profile, memorial submissions, memories and uploaded photos.

To start, use the button below and send the prepared message. If your device has no email app, write to together@skysbridge.org from your registered address with the subject “Delete my Skysbridge account”.

Start deletion request

Some information may be retained where required by law or to resolve security and abuse issues; if so, we will explain what is retained and why. See our Privacy page.
