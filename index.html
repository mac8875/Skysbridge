A place for every child among the stars

Every child deserves to be remembered.

Skysbridge is a quiet place for parents who carry a child in their heart but can no longer hold them in their arms. Here, families can preserve a name, a memory and a story, create a star, and decide for themselves what remains private or becomes part of A Sky Full of Stars. A place shaped by love, protected with care and held with dignity.

Why Sky is the first star

Born from Sky’s story

Sky’s life was short. The love he left behind has no end.

Skysbridge began with my son Sky—with the love he brought into my life and the certainty that no child should ever become only a date, a number or a silence.

Sky was deeply longed for, carried in hope and loved from the very first moment. We could not bring him home, but his place in our family never disappeared.

A Sky Full of Stars grew from that enduring love: a dignified place where families can keep a child’s name, memory and story close—in the way that feels right to them.
