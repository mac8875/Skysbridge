A place for every child among the stars

Every child deserves to be remembered.

Skysbridge is a digital memorial for children and babies who died, created for families living with child loss or baby loss. Bereaved parents can create a star, preserve memories and stories, and decide what remains private or becomes part of the public Wall of Stars. It is a protected place for grief, connection and enduring love.

Why Sky is the first star

Where Skysbridge began

Sky lived only a short time, but his light continues.

Skysbridge began with Sky—with the love he brought into my life and with the belief that no child should ever be reduced to a statistic.

Sky was our deeply longed-for child. He was conceived in love, carried in hope and loved from the very first moment. His story deserves a place that remains visible.

The Wall of Stars grew from that enduring love: a dignified place where other families can preserve a child's name, memory and story in the way that feels right to them.
