Our story

Sky lived only a short time, but his light continues.

Skysbridge began with Sky and with the wish that no child should ever be reduced to a statistic, and no parent should feel invisible in grief.

Grief does not affect everyone in the same way. Over time, we discovered that we carried our pain differently. Our paths eventually separated, but our love for Sky never did.

Skysbridge was born from that enduring love and from the hope that other families might find understanding sooner than we did.
