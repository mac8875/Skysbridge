Our story

Sky lived only a short time, but his light continues.

Skysbridge began with Sky—with the love he brought into my life and with the belief that no child should ever be reduced to a statistic, and no parent should ever feel unseen in grief.

Sky was our deeply longed-for child. He was conceived in love, carried in hope, and loved from the very first moment. Although grief shaped us in different ways and our lives eventually took separate paths, the love we share for Sky has never changed.

For me, Skysbridge grew from that enduring love and from the hope that other families might find understanding sooner than we did—so they feel less alone, better supported, and free to remember their child in their own way.
